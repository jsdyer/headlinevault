READ THE RECORD CAREFULLY

Evidence, with context.

Pages, not verified claims

Results are digitized newspaper pages, not individual articles. OCR can misread names, omit words or match unrelated columns. Opening excerpts are not guaranteed to contain your query. Read the scan before quoting a story.

A partial historical record

The collection covers selected U.S. newspapers. It can contain reporting about Canada, but is not a Canadian newspaper archive. Result counts describe this search and this collection, not how often something actually happened.

Your research trail

Saved captures have retrieval dates. Live results can change as the archive grows. Pins and imported receipts stay in this tab unless you export them. Images load from Library of Congress; your search fields are sent there only when you search.

Open sources, editable defaults

No account or API key is needed. Change web/config.json to add starting searches; use any supported terms in the form. The repository guide explains refreshing the saved captures.
